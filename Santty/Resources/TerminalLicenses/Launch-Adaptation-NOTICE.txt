NativeLocalLocale / NativeLocalLocaleName (+Tables) adapt the pure locale
conversion policy in libghostty-spm revision
1d84cf66fe29abd1f24f10b88dcfc25882cf68ee, Configuration/TerminalLocaleEnvironment.swift
and TerminalLocaleName{,+Tables}.swift. The native version returns explicit
child environment values; it removes configureEnvironment/global setenv and
never calls ghostty_init or links GhosttyTerminal/GhosttyKit. No libintl is linked.

The independent session owner retains SwiftTerm standalone LocalProcess reuse
and existing PTY patches described in PTY-Adaptation-NOTICE.txt. The local launch
factory and close barrier do not reproduce the old core's complete shell
integration or Ghostty terminfo resource policy. Production user environment
and login shell are explicitly distinct from private lab startup.
